# 0005. License: FSL-1.1-ALv2 with trademark policy

- **Status:** Accepted (pending legal review)

## Context

Offgrid is meant to be inspected, run and modified by the households and developers who use it. It also must not be turned into a competing commercial service by others. Fully open licenses allow that. Closed licenses block the trust that self-hosting needs.

## Decision

- The software is source-available under the Functional Source License, Version 1.1, Apache 2.0 Future (FSL-1.1-ALv2). It is not open source.
- Users may run, modify and redistribute it for any purpose except offering a competing commercial product or service.
- Each release converts to Apache-2.0 two years after it is published.
- The name and logo are governed by [TRADEMARK.md](../../TRADEMARK.md).

The full terms are in [LICENSE](../../LICENSE), and attributions are in [NOTICE](../../NOTICE).

## Consequences

- Contributors sign off their commits under the DCO (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)).
- Every source file carries the SPDX header for FSL-1.1-ALv2.
- The license text must be reviewed by counsel before the first public release. Until then this record stays pending legal review.
